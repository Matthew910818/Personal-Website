Beyond Academics 🏀

Beyond academics and research, I enjoy playing basketball, exploring new experiences, and challenging myself both physically and mentally. Whether through sports, outdoor adventures, or community service, I enjoy stepping outside my comfort zone and learning from different experiences. These activities have taught me the importance of perseverance, teamwork, and giving back to society.

Volunteer Works

Green Ark Aboriginal Care Association

I participated in a volunteer program organized by the Green Ark Aboriginal Care Association, where we distributed essential supplies, including rice, clothing, food, and daily necessities, to families in rural communities in Taiwan. Visiting these families in person gave me the opportunity to better understand their daily lives and reminded me to appreciate what I have. Seeing the smiles on their faces as they received the supplies also helped me realize the joy and sense of fulfillment that come from helping others. This experience motivated me to continue giving back to society and making a positive difference in others' lives.

Teach For Taiwan

The goal of this volunteer program was to raise funds to support educational resources for children in rural communities. Our main responsibility was to approach people on the streets, explain the purpose of the program, and ask for donations. Through this experience, I learned to overcome my fear of rejection and step outside my comfort zone. Knowing that I was contributing to a meaningful cause gave me the courage to approach strangers without worrying about embarrassment or how others might perceive me. Most importantly, it taught me to stay focused on my goals and not let others' judgments discourage me when I believe in what I am doing.

Freshman Orientation Camp

Camp Leader

I served as the leader of a university freshman orientation camp involving over 100 participants. I was responsible for coordinating the organizing team, planning activities, managing logistics, and raising approximately $3,000 in sponsorships to support the event. Leading a team toward a common goal was both challenging and rewarding. Organizing this event strengthened my leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills while learning the importance of teamwork and responsibility. Seeing everyone enjoy the event gave me a great sense of accomplishment and appreciation for what we achieved together.

Outdoor Activities

Outdoor activities help me stay healthy and relieve stress from my daily routine. From running half-marathons and climbing mountains to open-water swimming, I enjoy challenging myself both physically and mentally. These activities have strengthened my perseverance and taught me to embrace uncertainty. Even when the journey becomes physically or mentally demanding, I push myself to keep going and accomplish the goals I have set.

Military Service

Mandatory Military Service | Taiwan

During my mandatory military service, I was assigned to a police station, where I had the opportunity to see different sides of society and interact with people from various backgrounds. I learned how to communicate with different kinds of people, handle unfamiliar situations, and better understand the challenges others face. Military service also strengthened my sense of discipline, responsibility, and the importance of following rules and procedures.

One particularly memorable experience was participating in an Urban Resilience Exercise, which simulated an air raid emergency. During the exercise, civilians were required to stay indoors or seek designated air-raid shelters. I had the opportunity to assist with directing people to safe locations and ensuring that they followed the emergency procedures. It was a unique experience that allowed me to take on an active role in a public safety exercise.
