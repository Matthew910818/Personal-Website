Publications

Seg the HAB: Language-Guided Geospatial Algae Bloom Reasoning and Segmentation

NeurIPS 2025 Workshop on Tackling Climate Change with Machine Learning

Harmful algal blooms pose significant threats to aquatic ecosystems, making accurate monitoring essential. In this project, our team developed ALGOS, a multimodal framework that combines visual representations and language reasoning to identify harmful algal blooms and estimate their severity from satellite imagery. We also developed a GeoSAM-assisted annotation pipeline with human evaluation to generate reliable segmentation masks. Our approach achieved a class IoU of 0.6493, a global IoU of 0.5969, and reduced severity estimation MSE from 3.868 to 2.984 compared with the LLaVA-7B baseline. This work was accepted at the NeurIPS 2025 Workshop on Tackling Climate Change with Machine Learning.

Paper: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.18751

BargAIn: Agentic AI for Healthcare Debt Negotiation

Read project report (PDF)

UC Berkeley | CS194: Large Language Model Agents

Medical bills and insurance policies can be complicated to understand and negotiate. To address this challenge, our team developed BargAIn, an agentic AI system designed to assist users with medical bill analysis and healthcare debt negotiation. We implemented a multi-agent workflow using LangGraph, with specialized agents responsible for document analysis, insurance evaluation, legal compliance considerations, and negotiation strategies. By integrating retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with Qdrant, the system retrieves relevant medical billing and insurance information to support its recommendations and facilitate negotiation through voice and email interactions.

AI Cup Hackathon: Document Question Answering with LLMs

7th Place Among 487 Teams | 2024

Our team developed an AI-powered document question-answering system designed to extract and interpret information from lengthy PDF documents. We combined optical character recognition (OCR), text retrieval, and multiple large language models, including GPT, Gemini, Claude, and Llama, to generate answers grounded in document content. To improve reliability, we designed a voting and verification mechanism that compared responses across models and incorporated previous false-positive cases through few-shot prompting. Our system achieved 92.39% answer accuracy, placing 7th among 487 participating teams.

GenAI Stars Hackathon: AI-Powered Sports Video Analysis

Watch project demo on YouTube

Semifinalist & Merit Award Among 836 Teams | 2024

Our team developed an AI-powered sports video analysis platform that automatically identifies important moments and generates highlight clips. We integrated YOLOv8 for player detection, K-Means for team classification, and ByteTrack for multi-object tracking. To identify meaningful moments, we incorporated Whisper for speech transcription and text embeddings for segment retrieval, along with GPT-4 for video summarization. We combined these components into a Flutter application, demonstrating how computer vision and language models can work together to automate sports video analysis.
